# Draft Hair Intake

`hair_purchase` depends on hair_supplier and mail. Hair Purchasing → Hair Intakes
provides sequenced draft headers and multiple classification/weight lines.
Buyers (including Managers/Admin) create/edit company intakes; public, portal and
unrelated internal users have no access. Headers are retained; draft lines may be
removed. Standard chatter supports notes and attachment evidence.

All weights are kg at the confirmed 0.001 kg resolution. Odoo rounds entered
values to three decimal places. Payable kg is gross less tare, waste, moisture
and other deductions. Finite, nonnegative input and deductions not exceeding
gross are enforced on the server. Binary-float cancellation is avoided with
Decimal subtraction of the rounded quantities. Header totals update with lines.
Calculated values cannot be forged through create/write RPC calls.

Seller and classification relations must belong to the purchase company.
Company changes after adding lines are rejected to retain relation integrity.
References are generated by ir.sequence, unique per company and protected from
manual changes. Currency follows company currency for drafts.

This feature supports draft intake only. Pricing, inspection, commercial
confirmation, payments and stock actions will follow after their dependencies
and business authorization policy are established. No artificial commercial
transitions or prices are introduced.

Run `ruff check .` and `python3 scripts/test_addons.py`. Tests cover multi-line
calculations at 1 g resolution, deductions, nonfinite/negative data, relations,
company isolation, unauthorized access, lifecycle/derived-field protection and
view loading in an isolated database.
